# Realistic Experience Project

## Overview

This project is to develop and provide a standard, robust, and realistic experience for new players through a curated and fine-tuned selection of mods. Provide a cohesive and stable game environment for players to experience the best of what the modding community can offer.

## Goals

These goals are to be achieved primarily through the curation of existing mods, and the voluntary participation of the modding community.
1. Deliver a game experience balanced between increased realism and playability 
2. Ensure the baseline experience is stable and does not introduce significant bugs or crashes
3. Increase the breadth of available unit types and weapons across different Cold War and post-Cold War eras and nations
4. Standardize unit and weapons behaviors and performance metrics based on the best publicly-known data and observations
5. Provide new and/or enhanced realistic mechanics and play experience beyond what the base game is expected to provide
   1. Expanded ESM, EW, Radar and Sonar game mechanics
   2. Improved weapons behavior and mechanics
6. Include at least one, preferably multiple dynamic campaigns
7. Additional functionality to enhance the player experience

## Approach

The voluntary participation of the modding community will be key to achieve this. Multiple mods would be curated, and functionally and technically de-conflicted.

Participating modders would coordinate to help achieve these goals.

Delivery would occur through a single, separate "easy install" mod, whose goal is to automatically install, configure and tune as needed, and automatically order participating mods in the Mod Manager. The name of this future mod is to be determined.

## Foreseeable Challenges

### Scope

The scope of this effort would span the breadth of the Cold War and the post-Cold War era. This is a very large scope, with a multi-year timeline, that will likely need to be broken down into smaller, more manageable phases.

### Performance Baselines

A standardized performance baseline would be developed for units and weapons (Tofu of Pacific 2027 has started on this effort). This is particularly challenging for the post-cold war era, and will likely end up with "best reasonable estimate" performance metrics based on a variety of data and observational sources. These newest units and weapons will also likely see the most significant performance changes over time.

All performance metrics should have sources and evidence to back them up. Under no circumstances are classified sources to be used; publicly-leaked classified data is still classified data.

### Mod De-confliction

Currently, there are many excellent mods that would be excellent candidates for inclusion into this program. But in some cases the functionality of these mods may conflict and interfere with each other. De-conflicting these mods will require careful coordination and tuning among the modders to ensure a cohesive and stable player experience.


### Mod Longevity

The modding community is a voluntary scene. Mod authors are not compensated for their work. Their ability and availability to maintain their mods is never guaranteed nor required in any way. However, mod longevity is a crucial part of this project's long-term success.

As such, a voluntary mod stewardship and maintenance foundation should be considered. The purpose would be to maintain key / critical mods whose original authors are no longer able to participate, and have voluntarily turned over their mods (for however long they choose) to this group. This topic is also discussed in the [Open for discussion](open-for-discussion.md) list.
